What is Cyber Security?

Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and personal information from unauthorized access, cyber attacks, and digital threats. It uses technologies, security methods, and safe online practices to keep data protected.

Why is Cyber Security Important?

Cyber Security is important because people and organizations store a large amount of sensitive information online. Strong security helps prevent data theft, financial losses, identity theft, and damage caused by cyber criminals. It also helps create a safer and more trusted digital environment.

Common Online Threats Today

Today, users face many online threats such as phishing attacks, malware, ransomware, password attacks, and social engineering. Cyber criminals use these methods to steal information, access accounts, and harm computer systems. Learning about these threats and following safe online habits can help protect digital information.
