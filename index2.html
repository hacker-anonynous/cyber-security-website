Global Cyber Attack Statistics

Cyber statistics reveal the increasing scale of online threats across the world. Around 95% of cyber attacks begin with phishing emails, making them one of the most common attack methods. On average, a cyber attack occurs every 39 seconds, targeting individuals, businesses, and organizations. In addition, millions of passwords are leaked every year due to data breaches and weak security practices. These statistics highlight the importance of staying informed, using strong passwords, enabling two-factor authentication, and following safe online habits to protect personal and business data.
